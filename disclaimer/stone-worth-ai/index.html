Stone & Coin Disclaimer

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Stone & Coin Worth AI provides AI-assisted, photo-based results for informational and educational purposes. Results are approximate, may be incomplete or incorrect, and are not guarantees of identity, authenticity, grade, condition, origin, treatment, composition, or value.

The app is not a laboratory, gemological, professional authentication, certified grading, or certified appraisal service.

Stone analysis disclaimer

  • Photo-based analysis is approximate, and visually similar rocks, minerals, and crystals can be difficult to distinguish.
  • Reliable identification may require physical examination or laboratory testing.
  • Gemstone authenticity, treatment, composition, quality, and geographic origin cannot be guaranteed from photos.
  • Any stone or gemstone value context is informational and is not a certified appraisal or guaranteed sale price.

Coin analysis disclaimer

  • Photo-based coin identification can be uncertain. The exact issue or variant may depend on date, mint, strike, composition, errors, varieties, edge details, and other evidence that may not be visible.
  • Preliminary condition observations are not an official grade, and the app is not a professional grading or authentication service.
  • Counterfeit detection is not guaranteed. A result must not be treated as confirmation that a coin is genuine.
  • Estimated market ranges and reference value ranges depend on available, relevant evidence. The app may report “No reliable estimate” when evidence is insufficient.
  • Coin prices vary with the exact issue, authenticity, grade, surfaces, demand, venue, timing, and market conditions.
  • An exceptional auction price does not imply that another coin of the same broad type, denomination, or year has similar value.

High-value decisions

Independently verify rare, unusual, or potentially high-value items before buying, selling, insuring, or making another significant decision. For coins, professional authentication and grading may be appropriate. For stones and gems, laboratory testing or professional gemological verification may be appropriate. You remain responsible for decisions made using app results.
