Coin Guide

Learn what can make a coin collectible

Learn which visible details are worth noticing before Coin Scanner analyzes the actual coin in your photos.

YR

Year & key dates

Certain years, lower-mintage issues, and important dates can be more collectible.

M

Mint marks

Small letters or symbols can identify where a coin was made and distinguish similar issues.

◎

Condition

Wear, damage, luster, and preservation can affect collectibility and value.

Ag

Composition

Clad, silver, gold, copper, and other materials can separate otherwise similar coins.

≠

Errors & varieties

Doubled dies, off-center strikes, and die varieties can matter — but not every error is valuable.

★

Commemorative coins

Special designs may honor events, people, anniversaries, places, or historic themes.

US

Historic U.S. coins

Morgan Dollars, classic cents, older half dollars, and other historic issues offer rich context.

🌐

World coins

Regional issues can use different denominations, languages, designs, and dating systems.

Important notes

A smart starting point, with honest limits

AI-assisted analysis can help you research a find, but it is not a professional appraisal, laboratory identification, authentication, or grading service.

What can affect a result

  • Photo quality and lighting
  • Wear or damage
  • Unusual variants
  • Incomplete visible evidence

When to verify

For potentially high-value stones or coins, seek professional verification. Valuable coins may also warrant professional authentication or grading.
