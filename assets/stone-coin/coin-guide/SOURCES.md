## Presentation notes

- Images are displayed with `object-fit: contain`, so the coins are not distorted or materially cropped.
- The site adds only responsive sizing, a subtle frame, rounded corners, and a shadow; the photographic content itself is unchanged.
- These photographs are presented as educational identification examples, not grading references, endorsements, or value claims.
