# Historic auction sales: data and image provenance

Verified October 13, 2026. Sale-data provenance and image-license provenance are documented separately below. Auction-house photography was not downloaded or reused for the cards. Every displayed photograph is labeled on-page as a representative image of the coin type and must not be understood as a photograph of the specific auction lot.

## Sale data

### 1933 Double Eagle

- Realized price shown: $18,872,250 (commonly reported by Sotheby's as $18.9 million)
- Sale: June 8, 2021, Sotheby's, *Three Treasures – Collected by Stuart Weitzman*, lot 1
- Authoritative result: https://www.sothebys.com/en/articles/2021-historic-year-in-review
- Authoritative lot record: https://www.sothebys.com/en/buy/auction/2021/three-treasures-collected-by-stuart-weitzman/the-1933-double-eagle
- Verification note: Sotheby's identifies the $18.9 million result as the auction record for a coin in its 2021 review and documents the lot, sale, certification, and unique legal status in its lot record. The exact result is shown rather than the rounded headline figure.

### 1794 Flowing Hair Dollar

- Realized price: $10,016,875
- Sale: January 29, 2013, Stack's Bowers Galleries, January 2013 Americana Auction, lot 13094
- Authoritative result: https://stacksbowers.com/coin-resource-center/coin/1794-flowing-hair-dollar/
- Authoritative archive: https://archive.stacksbowers.com/
- Verification note: Stack's Bowers' Coin Resource Center and auction archive both report $10,016,875 for the Specimen-66 (PCGS), CAC example.

### 1787 Brasher Doubloon

- Realized price: $9,360,000
- Sale: January 21, 2021, Heritage Auctions, sale 1326, lot 3934
- Authoritative result: https://coins.ha.com/heritage-auctions-press-releases-and-news/world-s-most-valuable-gold-coin-doubloon-sets-nueve.36-million-world-record-at-heritage-auctions.s?releaseId=4081
- Verification note: Heritage's result announcement reports $9,360,000 and identifies the sold coin as the finest of seven known specimens.

### 1804 Draped Bust Silver Dollar

- Realized price: $7,680,000
- Sale: August 12, 2021, Stack's Bowers Galleries, August 2021 ANA Auction, lot 4114
- Authoritative result: https://stacksbowers.com/1804-draped-bust-class-i-silver-dollar-sold-for-7-million/
- Authoritative archive: https://archive.stacksbowers.com/
- Verification note: Stack's Bowers reports $7.68 million for the Proof-68 (PCGS) Class I Childs-Pogue specimen.

### 1913 Liberty Head Nickel

- Realized price: $4,560,000
- Sale: August 15, 2018, Stack's Bowers Galleries, August 2018 ANA Auction, lot 1096
- Authoritative result: https://stacksbowers.com/sbpressreleases/Finest-Known-1913-Nickel/
- Authoritative archive: https://archive.stacksbowers.com/
- Verification note: Stack's Bowers reports $4,560,000 for the Proof-66 (PCGS), CAC Eliasberg/Morton-Smith specimen and notes that five examples are known.

## Presentation and performance

- Images were downloaded as local assets from Wikimedia Commons rather than hot-linked.
- Images are lazy-loaded and asynchronously decoded below the fold.
- Cards use `object-fit: contain` to avoid distorting or materially cropping the coins.
- Auction-house result links are used for factual verification only; auction-house photographs are not copied.
