How it works

Follow every item from purchase to sale

Five steps, all in the Inventory tab of Flip Worth AI.

  1. 1

    Add to Inventory

    Save a scan to My Finds and tap Add to Inventory. With Marketplace IQ, tap Bought It on a deal and the purchase lands in Inventory for you.

    • From a saved scan
    • From Bought It in Marketplace IQ
  2. 2

    Record purchase information

    Enter what you paid and where you got it, so every item carries its own cost. Keep a receipt photo alongside the item photo from your scan.

    • Purchase price
    • Purchase date
    • Source or store
    • Notes
    • Receipt photo
  3. 3

    Track inventory and item status

    See everything you own in one list and update each item's status as it moves from the shelf to a listing. Filter and sort to find what you need.

    Record selling expenses — marketplace fees, shipping, packaging, repair or cleaning — against the item they belong to.

    • In stock
    • Listed
    • Sold
    • Refunded
    • Archived
  4. 4

    Mark items sold

    When an item sells, tap Mark as Sold and record the sale. Flip works out that item's net profit, ROI and margin from your purchase cost and expenses.

    If a sale is returned, mark it refunded or put the item back in stock.

    • Sale price
    • Sale date
    • Where you sold it
    • Notes
  5. 5

    Review dashboard totals and export records

    The Reseller Dashboard totals your results for this month, last month, this year or all time. Export your records to CSV when you need them in a spreadsheet or for your accountant.

    • Net Profit
    • Revenue
    • Selling Expenses
    • Current Inventory Cost
    • CSV export

Dashboard & export

See how the business is doing

The Reseller Dashboard adds up your sales, costs and expenses so you can see what you actually made — without building a spreadsheet by hand.

  • Period totals. Net profit, revenue, selling expenses and current inventory cost for the period you choose.
  • Averages. Items sold, active inventory, average profit per item, average ROI and average margin.
  • Based on real sales. Totals reflect sales you've recorded, not guesses about unsold items.
  • CSV export. Export your financial summary, sales and profit, expenses, and inventory.
