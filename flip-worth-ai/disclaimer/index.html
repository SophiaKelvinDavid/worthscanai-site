AI-generated estimates are for informational purposes only and are not a certified appraisal, authentication, or guaranteed resale price. Flip Worth AI does not guarantee resale value, sale price, profit, authenticity, rarity, or demand.

AI results may be incomplete or incorrect

AI-generated results can be incomplete, uncertain, or wrong. Photo quality affects results. Poor lighting, blurry images, or partial views of an item may reduce accuracy. Do not treat app results as definitive or authoritative.

Marketplace IQ and reseller calculations are estimates

Marketplace IQ does not predict what an item will sell for. The Target Resale Price used in Deal Decision is supplied by you. The following figures are estimates calculated from available listing information and your own assumptions:

  • Expected Profit
  • ROI
  • Margin
  • Break-even Sale Price
  • Max Buy

Actual results can differ. Marketplace fees, taxes, shipping, the actual selling price, and other costs can all be different from the amounts used in a calculation. If your assumptions change, the results change with them.

Listing information shown in Marketplace IQ — including in Opportunity Finder, For You, Deal Watch, and Marketplace Battle — comes from marketplaces and can be incomplete or out of date. Prices, availability, and listing details can change, and listings can end, at any time.
