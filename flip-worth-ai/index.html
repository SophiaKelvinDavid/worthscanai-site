Scan & Valuation

Know what you're holding before you pay for it

Snap a photo or upload one, and Flip gives you AI-assisted resale guidance in seconds — a quick read on whether an item may be worth flipping.

  • Scan or upload. Photograph an item at the thrift store, yard sale, or at home.
  • Estimated resale value. See a possible identification, an estimated resale range, and a resale signal.
  • Suggested next steps. Practical pointers to help you decide whether to walk away or dig deeper.
  • Plans that fit. Starter or Plus monthly scan plans, plus Scan Packs whenever you need extra scans.

Great for casual sellers and anyone starting out. Estimates are informational — not a certified appraisal, authentication, or guaranteed resale price.

Deal Radar: discovery built on your Buy Rules

Tell Flip what you buy and what you'll pay. Opportunity Finder checks your Buy Rules automatically, For You surfaces opportunities that match your Buy Rules, and Deal Alerts can notify you about eligible new matches. New Listings and Search are there when you want to dig in yourself.

Deal Decision: know the numbers before you buy

Review the seller's listing images, set your Target Resale Price, and see Expected Profit, ROI, Margin, Break-even Sale Price, and Max Buy — with a clear Buy Zone.

Watch, compare, then buy with confidence

Deal Watch monitors price and availability for you. Marketplace Battle puts options head-to-head. When you buy, Bought It hands the item straight to your inventory.

Your numbers, your call. Marketplace IQ doesn't predict what an item will sell for. It combines marketplace listing information with your own Target Resale Price to calculate deal economics — so you always know which assumptions drive the result.
