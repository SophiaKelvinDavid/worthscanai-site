What Flip Worth AI provides

Flip Worth AI is a reselling app for secondhand, thrift, garage sale, estate sale, marketplace, and used items. It has three product areas:

  • Scan & Valuation — AI-assisted scans of item photos that return a possible identification, an estimated resale range, and related resale signals.
  • Reseller Tools — inventory, purchase, expense, and sale records, a reseller dashboard, and CSV export, based on the information you enter.
  • Marketplace IQ — marketplace sourcing and deal-decision tools, including Buy Rules, Opportunity Finder, For You, Deal Alerts, Deal Decision, Deal Watch, Marketplace Battle, and Bought It.

Flip Worth AI is not a certified appraisal service, not an authentication service, and not an accounting, tax, legal, or financial advice service.

We do not warrant or guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or fitness for purpose of any scan result, and a scan result is not a guarantee of resale value, sale price, profit, authenticity, rarity, or demand.

Marketplace IQ and reseller calculations

Marketplace IQ calculations — such as Expected Profit, ROI, Margin, Break-even Sale Price, Max Buy, and Buy Zone — depend on marketplace listing information available to the app and on your own assumptions, including the Target Resale Price you enter. Marketplace IQ does not predict what an item will sell for. Listing information can be incomplete or out of date, and listings can change or end at any time.

Reseller Tools figures, such as net profit, ROI, and margin, are calculated from the purchase, expense, and sale information you record.
