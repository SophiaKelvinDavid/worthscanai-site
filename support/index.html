Stone & Coin Support

For Stone & Coin Worth AI (a WorthScan AI app) support, contact:

What we can help with

  • Stone Scanner: scan problems, photo quality, and questions about stone, mineral, or crystal results.
  • Coin Scanner: front/back photo problems, coin identification or result questions, and market or reference estimate questions.
  • Plans and allowances: total scan credits, Coin allowance, subscriptions, Scan Packs, and restoring purchases.
  • Account and app: sign-in, My Finds, privacy, account/data deletion, and other technical problems.

A Coin scan uses one total scan and one Coin allowance. A Stone scan uses one total scan. Scan Packs add total scans but do not add Coin allowance.

stoneworthai.support@gmail.com

To delete your account or request data deletion, see the Delete Account & Data page.

Please include

  • App name
  • Issue description
  • Device type
  • Account email used in app, if relevant

Including these details helps us respond more quickly and accurately.
